When do I have to order by?

Thursday 11:59 PM Pacific for that Sunday. The board shows a live countdown. A new board goes up every Friday. Add a weekly Thursday reminder to your calendar.

Which Sunday am I ordering for?

The first Sunday after the Thursday close. If you order on a Friday, Saturday or Sunday, the meals are for the following Sunday, not the one two days away; the checkout says so in plain words.

Can I mix 4 oz and 8 oz in one bag?

Not in the preview. One size and one style per bag keeps the kitchen fast and the price simple. Switching the size re-prices the whole bag. Sizes are sample options until Fina confirms hers.

How do the price breaks work?

In the sample ladder, at 5 meals every meal drops 50 cents, at 10 a dollar, at 15 a dollar fifty, and delivery is free at 15. The receipt tape counts down to the next break and shows the savings in dollars. Prices are a sample until Fina confirms hers.

Where do you deliver?

Sample fees: $4 around Downey, Bell Gardens and Long Beach (ZIPs starting 902 and 908), $7 across the rest of Los Angeles County, $9 in Orange County. Delivery runs Sunday 5:30 to 9:00 PM in the sample window. Everywhere else is pickup only; type your ZIP on the board to check.

Where and when is pickup?

Downey pickup, address and hours coming from Fina. The sample window is Sunday 5:30 to 8:30 PM. You pick a half hour at checkout and the bars show how busy each one usually is. Add the Sunday to your calendar from checkout so the meal is not left behind.

How do I pay?

Fina's is not taking payments yet; this site is a working preview. Nothing you type is sent or stored off your device.

How do I cancel?

Open your confirmation screen and press Cancel before Thursday. After the Thursday close the kitchen has already shopped and cooked for you, so orders are final.

Where are the calories and macros?

Coming from Fina. When they arrive they print on every tile and on the menu page, and the "under 500 calories" and "highest protein" rules in Plan my week switch on.

Where are the allergens?

On every tile and on the Menu page, from the dish names for now: shrimp is shellfish, pasta is wheat, Parm and cream are dairy, teriyaki and miso are soy. Full lists come with the confirmed menu. The Menu page filters leave out shellfish, fish, dairy, wheat, soy, tree nuts or egg.

What kind of food is it?

Fina's Meal Kitchen in Downey, California cooks American fusion, something for everyone: 57 dishes across chicken, beef, fish, shrimp, pasta and breakfast. Twelve are on the board each week.

Can I swap a side or a sauce?

No substitutions on dishes in the preview. Sauces are on the board as extras, two free with every five meals.
